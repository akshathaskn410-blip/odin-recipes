Chocolate-Cake

Description

Healthy and delicious, no egg, no maida chocolate cake recipe

Ingredients:

  • Wheat flour
  • Sugar
  • Baking soda
  • Baking Powder
  • Milk
  • Cocoa powder
  • oil/Butter

Steps:

  1. Add 1 cup of wheat flour, 1 and a half cup of sugar, 1/4 ts of baking powder, 1/2 ts of baking soda to mixing bowl and mix everything together
  2. Add boiled but cooled down milk to the mixture gradually until you reach the desired consistency
  3. Now, pour the mixture to the cake tray and airfry it untill it's completely cooked
  4. Check if it is properly baked or not using the toothpick method
  5. Now, pour the chocolate sauce on the cake and enjoy. For the chocolate sauce recipe, click the link below
